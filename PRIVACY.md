Privacy Policy for SampLift

Effective date: 1st May 2026

SampLift is a Chrome extension that allows users to capture audio from the active browser tab, trim audio loops, adjust tempo, apply effects, and export WAV files.

This Privacy Policy explains what information SampLift processes, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Information SampLift processes

SampLift may process the following information:

Browser tab audio:
When you choose to use SampLift, the extension captures audio from the active browser tab. This audio is processed locally in your browser so you can record, trim, loop, edit, and export it.

Local extension data:
SampLift may store local settings in your browser, including Pro activation status, app preferences, and related extension state.

Payment status:
SampLift uses ExtensionPay to check whether you have purchased SampLift Pro. ExtensionPay and Stripe may process payment-related information, such as your email address and payment status. SampLift does not directly process or store your full payment card details.

2. How SampLift uses information

SampLift uses information only to provide and improve its user-facing features, including:

• Capturing audio from the tab you choose
• Displaying and trimming waveforms
• Applying playback and audio effects
• Exporting WAV files
• Remembering local app settings
• Checking whether SampLift Pro is active

SampLift does not use your data for advertising.

3. Audio processing

Audio captured by SampLift is processed locally in your browser.

SampLift does not upload your recorded audio to SampLift-owned servers.

Exported WAV files are saved by you to your own device.

4. Payments

SampLift Pro payments are handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe.

When you purchase or restore SampLift Pro, ExtensionPay and Stripe may process information necessary to complete the transaction, verify payment status, prevent fraud, and provide payment support.

SampLift does not store your full credit card number or banking details.

You should review ExtensionPay’s and Stripe’s own privacy policies for details about how they handle payment information.

5. Data sharing

SampLift does not sell your personal information.

SampLift does not transfer your data to third parties except where necessary to provide the extension’s features, process payments, comply with law, prevent abuse, or maintain security.

Payment-related information is handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe as necessary to provide SampLift Pro purchases and payment verification.

6. Chrome Web Store Limited Use disclosure

SampLift’s use of information received from Chrome extension APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

SampLift only uses Chrome extension permissions and related data to provide or improve its single purpose: capturing, editing, looping, and exporting browser tab audio.

SampLift does not use Chrome extension data for advertising.

SampLift does not sell Chrome extension data.

SampLift does not allow humans to read user data unless necessary for security, legal compliance, user support, or abuse investigation.

7. Data retention

Audio recordings are held in the browser during your SampLift session and are not uploaded to SampLift-owned servers.

Local extension settings may remain stored in your browser until you clear extension data, uninstall the extension, or reset settings.

Payment records may be retained by ExtensionPay and Stripe according to their own policies and legal obligations.

8. Security

SampLift is designed to process audio locally in your browser.

Payment processing is handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe using their payment infrastructure.

You should only install SampLift from trusted distribution sources, such as the official Chrome Web Store listing.

9. Children’s privacy

SampLift is not directed to children under 13. SampLift does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. When changes are made, the effective date above will be updated.
