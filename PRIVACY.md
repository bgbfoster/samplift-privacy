Privacy Policy for SampLift

Effective date: 1st May 2026

SampLift is a Chrome extension that allows users to record audio from the active browser tab, trim audio loops, adjust tempo, apply effects, and export WAV files.

This Privacy Policy explains what SampLift processes, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Single purpose
SampLift’s single purpose is to help users record, trim, loop, process, and export audio from a browser tab they choose.

SampLift does not operate as a background recorder. SampLift does not start recording until the user opens the extension and starts recording.

2. Information SampLift processes
SampLift may process the following information:

Browser tab audio
When you choose to use SampLift, the extension can record audio from the active browser tab. This audio is processed locally in your browser so you can view a waveform, trim the audio, loop playback, apply effects, and export a WAV file.

SampLift does not upload your recorded audio to SampLift-owned servers.

Local extension data
SampLift may store local extension state in your browser, including Pro activation status, app preferences, and related settings.

Payment status
SampLift uses ExtensionPay to check whether SampLift Pro is active. ExtensionPay and Stripe may process payment-related information, such as your email address, payment status, transaction details, and information needed for fraud prevention and payment support.

SampLift does not directly process or store your full payment card number, CVC, or banking details.

3. How SampLift uses information
SampLift uses information only to provide its user-facing features, including:

Recording audio from the active tab after user action
Displaying the recorded waveform
Trimming audio
Looping playback
Applying tempo and audio effects
Exporting WAV files
Remembering local settings
Checking whether SampLift Pro is active
SampLift does not use your data for advertising.

SampLift does not sell your personal information.

SampLift does not use Chrome extension data to determine creditworthiness, lend money, or make eligibility decisions.

4. Audio processing
Audio recorded by SampLift is processed locally in your browser.

SampLift does not upload recorded audio to SampLift-owned servers.

Exported WAV files are saved by you to your own device.

Audio may remain available in the SampLift session while the extension window is open. Closing the extension or clearing the recording removes the in-session recording from the app.

5. Payments
SampLift Pro payments are handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe.

When you purchase or restore SampLift Pro, ExtensionPay and Stripe may process information needed to complete the transaction, verify payment status, prevent fraud, and provide payment support.

SampLift does not store your full credit card number or banking details.

Please review ExtensionPay’s and Stripe’s privacy policies for information about how they handle payment-related data.

6. Data sharing
SampLift does not sell user data.

SampLift does not transfer user data to third parties except where necessary to provide the extension’s features, process payments, comply with law, prevent abuse, maintain security, or provide user support.

Payment-related information is handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe as necessary for SampLift Pro purchases and payment verification.

7. Chrome Web Store Limited Use disclosure
SampLift’s use of information received from Chrome extension APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

SampLift only uses Chrome extension permissions and related data to provide or improve its single purpose: recording, editing, looping, processing, and exporting audio from a user-selected browser tab.

SampLift does not use Chrome extension data for advertising.

SampLift does not sell Chrome extension data.

SampLift does not transfer Chrome extension data except as necessary to provide or improve SampLift’s user-facing features, comply with law, prevent abuse, maintain security, or process payments through ExtensionPay and Stripe.

SampLift does not allow humans to read user data unless necessary for security, legal compliance, user support, or abuse investigation.

8. Permissions
SampLift uses the following Chrome extension permissions:

tabCapture
Used to record audio from the active browser tab after the user intentionally opens SampLift and starts recording.

activeTab
Used so SampLift can work with the currently selected tab that the user intentionally chooses by clicking the extension toolbar icon.

storage
Used to save local extension state, Pro activation status, and user preferences.

extensionpay.com content script
Used so ExtensionPay can complete payment and restore-purchase callback handling for SampLift Pro. This is limited to extensionpay.com.

9. Data retention
Audio recordings are held locally during your SampLift session and are not uploaded to SampLift-owned servers.

Local extension settings may remain stored in your browser until you clear extension data, uninstall SampLift, or reset settings.

Payment records may be retained by ExtensionPay and Stripe according to their own policies and legal obligations.

10. Security
SampLift is designed to process recorded audio locally in your browser.

Payment processing is handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe using their payment infrastructure.

You should only install SampLift from trusted distribution sources, such as the official Chrome Web Store listing.

11. Responsible use
SampLift is intended for lawful, user-controlled audio sampling and editing.

Only record audio that you have the right to record and use.

SampLift does not bypass DRM, remove copy protection, access paid content without authorization, or download media files from websites.

12. Children’s privacy
SampLift is not directed to children under 13. SampLift does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. When changes are made, the effective date above will be updated.
